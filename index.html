Sprinter Ball

Created by YoungOfAfrica. Player 1: WASD, Shift to run, E to hide. Player 2: Arrow keys, / to run, Enter to hide. The seeker tags the hider by touching them.

How to Play

The seeker scores by catching the hider before the timer runs out. The hider scores by staying hidden until time is up.

Player 1 Move with WASD Run with Shift Hide with E
Player 2 Move with Arrow keys Run with / Hide with Enter

Only the hider can hide. Step into bushes, tents, crates, or sheds, then press the hide key.
